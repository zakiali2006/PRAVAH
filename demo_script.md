# PRAVAH - Voice-over Script (For the Speaker)

This script matches the `demo_recording_guide.md`. Read this aloud while your teammate follows the clicks in the video, or record the audio later and sync it to the video.

---

### Scene 1: Introduction & Landing Page (0:00 - 0:15)
**[Video starts on the landing page]**
"Hello everyone, we are presenting PRAVAH, an AI-powered Single Window Clearance system designed to revolutionize how businesses and investors interact with government departments. Let's walk through the platform."

### Scene 2: Trilingual Investor Dashboard (0:15 - 0:45)
**[Video logs in and shows Investor Dashboard]**
"First, we log in as an Investor. The dashboard gives a unified view of all applications, current statuses, and a dynamic roadmap. 
Because accessibility is critical, PRAVAH features full native trilingual support. 
**[Video toggles languages]**
With a simple toggle, the entire interface seamlessly switches between English, Marathi, and Hindi. 
**[Video opens Chatbot]**
We've also integrated an AI-powered chatbot that guides users through complex compliance questions instantly, breaking down regulatory barriers."

### Scene 3: DigiLocker & Document Vault (0:45 - 1:10)
**[Video navigates to Document Vault and fetches from DigiLocker]**
"To eliminate redundant data entry, PRAVAH features a highly secure Document Vault integrated with DigiLocker. 
Instead of uploading documents manually, the investor authorizes DigiLocker, and instantly, verified documents are pulled into the vault. This guarantees authenticity and saves significant time."

### Scene 4: Single Window Application (1:10 - 1:30)
**[Video opens Apply for Service and scrolls through the Factory License form]**
"When applying for a new service, such as a Factory License, PRAVAH acts as a true Single Window. 
The form automatically fetches the verified details from the Document Vault, meaning the investor doesn't have to re-enter their PAN, GST, or address. They simply review, fill in any service-specific details, and submit."

### Scene 5: Officer AI Dashboard & Workload Balancer (1:30 - 2:00)
**[Video logs in as Officer, shows dashboard and then Duplicate Alerts]**
"Now, let's look at the backend from a Department Officer's perspective. 
PRAVAH isn't just a portal; it's an AI-driven workload balancer. 
Instead of a simple chronological queue, applications are triaged by AI. 
**[Video clicks on Duplicate Alerts]**
Furthermore, the system automatically detects potential fraud. Here, our deduplication engine has flagged identical or highly similar applications—saving officers hours of manual cross-referencing."

### Scene 6: AI Risk Triage (2:00 - 2:20)
**[Video clicks Process on an application to open the Action Required Modal]**
"When an officer processes an application, they don't just see raw forms. They see our AI Risk Triage Analysis. 
The AI cross-references the fetched documents, assigns a Trust Score, and evaluates Entity Reputation. This allows the officer to instantly confidently approve low-risk applications, drastically reducing clearance times."

### Scene 7: Policy Admin / Analytics (2:20 - 2:50)
**[Video logs in as Policy Maker / Admin]**
"Finally, we log in as a Policy Admin. High-level decision-makers get a macro-view of state-wide clearance rates and bottlenecks. 
**[Video shows CAF Builder/Service Manager]**
We've also included a dynamic CAF (Common Application Form) Builder. When regulations change, admins can instantly deploy new forms across the system without touching a single line of code. 

Thank you for watching our demonstration of PRAVAH—streamlining compliance through AI and interoperability."
