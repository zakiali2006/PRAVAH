**(Start speaking as the video begins on the landing page)**

If an entrepreneur wants to know about the platform's key features, they simply click a pre-set question. The AI instantly provides a clear, formatted summary of our highlights, like the Central Vault and Smart Triage.

## 🎙️ Scene 2: PRAVAH Central Vault & Business Profile
**(Speak when the user logs in and navigates to 'My Business')**

"Here, we log in as an **Investor**. 

Traditionally, businesses have to upload the same documents and enter the same details for every single application. We've solved this with the **PRAVAH Central Vault**. 

The investor only needs to set up their 'Business Profile' once. They input their core entity details like PAN, CIN, GSTIN, and Date of Incorporation. Once saved, this data acts as a single, verified source of truth stored securely in the backend, completely eliminating redundant data entry across different government departments."

## 🎙️ Scene 3: AI Pre-fill & Application Process
**(Speak when the user clicks 'Services' and goes to the Application form)**

"Let’s see the Central Vault in action. When the investor applies for a service, like a Factory Licence, they don’t have to manually type their details again. 

Notice the **PRAVAH AI Pre-fill** banner. With a single click, our system connects to the Central Vault, verifies the KYC, and intelligently maps the database fields directly into the application form. 

*Pause slightly while the animation runs.*

The AI successfully auto-fills all the required fields, marking them with blue 'AI Filled' badges. This reduces application drafting time from hours down to literal seconds, minimizing human error."

## 🎙️ Scene 6: Officer Dashboard (Smart Triage & Risk Scoring)
**(Speak when the user logs in as the Officer)**

"Now, let's switch perspectives. We are logging in as a **Government Officer**. 

Officers deal with thousands of applications. To help them, we implemented an **AI-driven Smart Triage and Risk Scoring engine**. As you can see on the dashboard, every incoming application is automatically assigned a risk score—Low, Medium, or High—based on the applicant's history and data consistency. 

When the officer opens an application, they aren't just blindly verifying documents. Our OCR and AI verification models cross-check the submitted documents against the Central Vault, providing a confidence score. This empowers the officer to fast-track low-risk applications and focus their manual scrutiny only on high-risk cases."
